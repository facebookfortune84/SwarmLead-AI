# Product Hunt Launch Plan — SwarmOS (Genesis)

Launch: **Monday, 00:01 PST.** Everything below is executable by the swarm and the
founder. The goal is not just "good launch" — it is **traffic that converts to the
first paying customer this week**. The product already has a live Stripe checkout,
so the only thing standing between a visitor and revenue is traffic + trust.

---

## What the product actually is (for PH copy)

An **agentic OS that launches a real business while you talk to it** — full-duplex
voice agent (barge-in, sub-200ms), 15-agent workforce that runs outreach, SEO and
follow-ups, and zero-to-provisioned business scaffolding. Not a chatbot wrapper —
a working system, live on the landing page **right now**.

## Pre-launch (Today → Sunday)

- [ ] Create/verify Maker profile on Product Hunt (real photo, bio with link).
- [ ] Join a PH launch group (e.g. "Build in Public", "Launch") — comment genuinely for 48h.
- [ ] Recruit **20–30 early supporters**: DM 10 friends + 15–20 founders you've
      helped. Ask: *"I'm launching on PH Monday — could you upvote in the first hour?"*
- [ ] Post teasers today: LinkedIn + X. Hook: *"I built the OS that launched itself."*
- [ ] Prepare **5 gallery images** + a **45-second demo video** (screen-record the voice
      agent answering + a business being provisioned). Caption each image.
- [ ] **The live URL is your secret weapon**: link the PH post to the landing page
      with the voice agent front-and-center. Visitors who "play" with it stay.

## Launch day (Monday)

- [ ] Post at **00:01 PST** (not before). Category: **Artificial Intelligence**.
- [ ] **First comment within 60 seconds** with the hook + demo video + link (see copy).
- [ ] **Answer every comment within 5–10 minutes for the first 2 hours.** Speed is
      the ranking signal that matters most on launch day.
- [ ] Reply to every single upvote-thank you individually (no copy-paste walls).
- [ ] At hour 6 and hour 20, post an update comment with a concrete result
      (a screenshot of a provisioned business, a metric).

## Post-launch (Tue–Wed)

- [ ] Thank every supporter. Ask for feedback, not more upvotes.
- [ ] Turn the best PH comment into a LinkedIn post + directory copy.
- [ ] Submit to all directories in `data/acquisition_directories.json`.
- [ ] Send the **Business Skeleton Generator** to every PH commenter who "sounds like
      a founder" — that's the email-capture lead magnet that feeds the growth loop.

## Profit note (honest)

Product Hunt alone does not guarantee sales. It is a **traffic engine**: PH-adjacent
tools average a few thousand visits on a good launch. With a live checkout and a
converting landing page, even a **1–2% visitor→signup→pay** path turns ~1,000 engaged
visitors into **10–20 signups and the first few paid seats**. The loop then has real
leads to draft against. That is the honest math — no guarantees, real leverage.
